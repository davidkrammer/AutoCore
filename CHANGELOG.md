# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-09)

First public release.

- Mirror an iPhone on the Mac through a built-in AirPlay receiver, started
  from the Mac with one click, and reconnect by itself after a restart.
- Control it over Wi‑Fi through WebDriverAgent: clicks, drags and the Mac
  keyboard on the mirror; taps, swipes, typing, opening apps and going Home
  from code, with human-like variation.
- Read the screen with Vision text recognition in a self-repairing helper.
- One window per iPhone; the panel can be hidden.
- The Remote for iPhone: live picture, tabs and settings, taps and typing,
  paired with a 6-digit code and end-to-end encrypted.
- Demo tools: open apps, Home, swipe, type, read the screen, find and tap.
- Build your own app on AutoCore with `AppBrand` and `WindowTools`.
- English and German.
